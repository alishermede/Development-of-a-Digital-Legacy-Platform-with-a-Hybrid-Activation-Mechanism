## Interpretation (interviewer's conclusions, not the respondent's words)

- A typical user without technical background: data on the phone, passwords on paper with no known location. She knows the problem from a real case but has not done anything herself.
- For her the main goal of the service is that close people get what is needed. Privacy is a limit: access to "really needed" data, not to everything.
- The concept is understandable overall, while encryption and key splitting cause confusion. Such users need simple explanations.
- The longest pause after confirmation of the three interviews (about a month). She links it directly to protection against mistakes.
- WhatsApp as a notification channel was not mentioned in our requirements.
- She asked for the ability to cancel, which matches the cancellation requirement in the waiting period.

## New requirements or ideas

- Ability to cancel the release (already in the requirements).
- Pass only the needed data, not everything.
- A clear instruction for users without a technical background.
- WhatsApp as a channel (check in DIP-13).

## What remains unclear

- What exactly she counts as "needed" data.
- Why she chose a month of waiting rather than a shorter period.
- What prevents her from writing the passwords down for relatives now.
