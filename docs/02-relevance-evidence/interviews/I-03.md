## Interpretation (my conclusions, not the respondent's words)

- The only respondent who already has a solution (the Google feature, a master password on paper in a safe). The reason is a painful personal experience: she saw the difficulty of getting access to the email of a friend's deceased husband.
- This confirms both that the problem is real and that Google has a working competitor worth including in the system comparison (DIP-10). The difference she herself pointed out: in our service the key is split between several people.
- For her a false activation is acceptable if only the necessary data is opened, and a failed activation is the worst. This is the only direct comparison of the two errors in the three interviews.
- Periods: 10 days before the alarm, about 5 days after the confirmation, a check-in once a month. The spread between respondents is large.
- The answer to question 14 is incomplete, there is no amount. It is not enough to assess willingness to pay.

## New requirements or ideas

- Open only the necessary data, not everything.
- The trusted person confirms the status and passes the key, nothing more.
- Consider Google "Inactive Account Manager" as a competitor in the comparison.
